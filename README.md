# 🏥 Syam Multispeciality Hospital Operational Dashboard (Streamlit)

A comprehensive, multi-page operational dashboard designed to provide **Syam Multispeciality Hospital (Bhimavaram)** with an Executive Operational Summary and deep-dive analytics across key functional departments, including Clinical Operations, HR, Pharmacy, and Laboratory Services.

This dashboard is built on **Streamlit**, making it highly interactive and easy to deploy for internal operational review and strategic decision-making.

---

## ✨ Dashboard Structure and Features

The application is structured into a main Executive Summary page and four detailed departmental deep-dive pages:

### 1. Executive Operational Summary (SD1.PNG)
* **Purpose:** Provides a high-level overview of the hospital's performance.
* **Key Indicators:** Displays aggregated values for **Total Patient Footfall**, **Total Converted Cases (INR)**, **Total Cost Over Time**, and **Total Laboratory Cost (INR)**.
* **Core Insights:** Includes trends like **Patient Satisfaction Composite Trend** and a breakdown of **Department vs. Total Financial Contribution**.
* **Specialties:** A quick-access panel showing **Available Services and Specialties** (e.g., Cardiology, Nephrology, Orthopaedics).

### 2. Clinical Services & Quality Dashboard (SD2.PNG)
* **Focus:** Core operational and quality metrics for patient care.
* **Metrics:** Tracks **Patient Stay Score**, **Complication Rate**, and **Bed Occupancy (%)**.
* **Deep Dives:** Includes trends for **Admission Volume**, **Top 10 Diagnoses by Cost**, **Complication Rate Trend**, and **OT Utilization Funnel**.

### 3. HR Workforce & Analytics (SD3.PNG)
* **Focus:** Human Resources and staff performance.
* **Metrics:** Shows **Total Employees**, **Staff Absenteeism**, and **Recruitment Score**.
* **Deep Dives:** Features analytics on **Overtime by Department & Shift**, **Staff Category Distribution** (Pie Chart), **Satisfaction by Role** (Box Plot), and **Tenure vs. Absenteeism** (Scatter Plot).

### 4. Laboratory Services & Ancillary Efficiency (SD4.PNG)
* **Focus:** Efficiency and quality of laboratory and ancillary services.
* **Metrics:** Tracks **Average TAT (Hrs)**, **Total Rejection Rate (%)**, and **Test Volume by Department**.
* **Deep Dives:** Visualizes **Test Volume Trend**, **Top Rejection Reasons**, **TAT vs. Cost per Test**, and **Instrument Utilization**.

### 5. Pharmacy Services & Medication Safety (SD5.PNG)
* **Focus:** Pharmacy operations, inventory, and safety.
* **Metrics:** Displays **Core Pharmacy Performance Metrics** like volume and cost.
* **Deep Dives:** Analyzes **Dispensing Safety Events**, **Revenue & Volume Trend**, **High-Cost Drug Utilization**, and **Avg. Dispensing vs. TAT (Mins)**.
